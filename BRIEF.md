# Postigent — landing page brief

- **Business:** Postigent, a social media scheduling app (built on Postiz, AGPL-3.0), owned by
  WHATSBULK TECHNOLOGIES LIMITED (RC 9559554), Nigeria.
- **What it does:** connect social/chat channels, plan and schedule posts on one calendar,
  publish automatically, collaborate with a team, see analytics.
- **Audience:** small businesses and creators first (Nigeria-first, global-friendly), with a
  dedicated section for agencies and social media managers who run clients' accounts.
- **Primary goal:** sign-ups → https://app.postigent.com/auth. Secondary: sign in.
- **Secondary goal:** be a public homepage that satisfies Google OAuth and Meta app review:
  describe the app and how it uses connected-account data, link Privacy / Terms / Data deletion,
  show the legal owner and a contact address.
- **Sections:** Nav, Hero, Channels strip, Features, How it works, For agencies,
  Your data (privacy & Google Limited Use), FAQ, Final CTA, Footer. **No pricing section.**
  No testimonials or user counts (new product; nothing fake).
- **Style:** dark, matching the app (near-black + burnt orange).
- **Assets:** user's logo (mark + dark-mode lockup); channel icons from the app.
- **Links:** Privacy https://app.postigent.com/privacy.html · Terms /terms.html ·
  Data deletion /data-deletion.html · Contact info@postigent.com · Source github.com/Oladapodaniel/postigent
- **Hosting:** Vercel, apex domain postigent.com (DNS on Vercel).
